Tomorrow's Setup — Three Paths Into the Open

Levels that matter: today's close at $765.53 is the pivot. Above $771 = continuation regime; below $761 = reversal regime; chop in between = pin mode.

Bull path
PCE cooperates, NVDA validates the capex, and SPY takes 770 with the software damage contained to INTU

Bull path: core PCE prints at or under ~3.3% at 8:30, the 10-year extends its two-day easing with crude still near $81, and the market treats the INTU guide as company-specific — a Mailchimp and Desktop problem plus a chosen TurboTax price cut — rather than a read on the software layer. IGV gaps down and gets bought; SOXX holds its +2% session; SPY works above 766 during the cash day and sets up 770, the level where dealer hedging flips from amplifying to damping. Then NVDA delivers on revenue near $92B with a Rubin ramp commentary that frames the >15% system price increase as pricing power rather than customer strain, and the stock clears its 5.4-7% implied to the upside. In that configuration the AI trade re-collapses into a single narrative instead of two, MRVL Thursday becomes a follow-through rather than a fresh test, and Warsh Friday meets a market that can absorb a neutral-to-firm tone. Expression: keep the front-week convexity through Friday regardless — it is the hedge, not the position — hold semis without adding, and buy quality software weakness only after the NVDA print, not before it.

Base case
Software gaps down, semis hold, and SPY chops 766-770 waiting for 4:20 PM

Base case: the INTU hole opens and stays open, dragging IGV and the application-software complex 1-3% without generalizing to the platform names, while SOXX holds most of today's gain because NVDA is a same-day binary nobody wants to be short into. SPY spends the cash session inside 766-770 — the band it just reclaimed — with the 8:30 data absorbed and neither PCE nor GDP carrying enough forward information to move it, since July data predates both the Canadian tariff escalation and the sanctions package. Breadth stays as thin as it was today: green index, negative advance-decline underneath, defensives quietly bid on the back of the Consumer Confidence expectations miss. Crude holds $80-83 depending on whether the ceasefire report firms or fades, gold consolidates near $4,700-4,750, Bitcoin works the $78-80k shelf, and the 10-year stays inside 4.65-4.75%. The reliable riser in this path is the front of the vol curve, because 15.45 cannot hold through the last two hours before a print that is $280B of implied cap. Posture: small in direction, long front-week convexity, and let 4:20 PM do the work.

Bear path
The INTU guide generalizes, PCE runs hot, and 766 fails with no cushion beneath it

Bear path has two triggers that can fire independently and are worse together. First, the market decides INTU was information rather than idiosyncrasy: a company beating both lines and then cutting its FY27 EPS bar ~16% because it must reprice against AI competition is a template, not an exception, and it gets applied to the entire application-software complex — ZM's light profit guide against +184% AI Companion adoption is the corroborating datapoint. That compresses the multiple on the layer that was supposed to monetize AI capex, which is a direct problem for the layer selling it, and NVDA reports into it. Second, core PCE surprises above ~3.3% at 8:30, the 10-year reverses two days of easing back through 4.75%, and the one-in-three September hike gets repriced higher three days before Warsh speaks. Either path runs into the same structural problem: the dealer long-gamma cushion settled away at August expiration, so hedging flows amplify rather than absorb. SPY just reclaimed 766 on a final-hour ceasefire headline that neither government has confirmed — a low-quality print to defend. Losing it puts 762 in play, 758 is the trapdoor, 754 sits under it with no mechanism between, and the break would be travelling into an NVDA print the same evening. Add the consumer: expectations at a seven-month low behind a 30% DKS gap means a soft Q4 guide from any large retailer generalizes immediately.

Bottom Line

The session story: The index got exactly the repair the morning thesis argued for, and every control underneath it failed. But the last hour was bought on an unconfirmed report that Washington and Tehran were nearing a fresh ceasefire, the same headline that took WTI to $81.03, -1.61% for a third straight down session, and Hegseth was publicly hedging it the same afternoon. Behind the tape, two things broke. August Consumer Confidence printed 89.4 against 90.2 expected — the lowest since January — with the expectations sub-index down 7.8% even as current conditions improved for the first time in four months: fine today, frightened about tomorrow. DKS closed -30.68% at $124.31, roughly three times its 10.5% implied move, and took TGT -4.3% with it. Breadth never confirmed the green print — 973 Russell advancers against 1,103 decliners. Then the bell rang and the software control experiment the morning had flagged failed outright: INTU beat FQ4 on both lines and guided FY27 EPS to $22.88-$23.12 against a $27.30 consensus and FQ1 to $2.44-$2.48 against $4.02, naming softer Mailchimp, a declining Desktop ecosystem and a deliberate decision to take less revenue per TurboTax customer to defend share — and fell 9-11% on top of a -3.4% session, now roughly -47% YTD. ZM -4% with AI Companion paid users +184% and a light profit guide, NCNO -10%, SYRE -16%. So Monday's bifurcation inverted twice in one day: hardware was liquidated and software held; then hardware ripped and software was taken apart on numbers. Cross-asset rotated with it — gold reversed up 0.75% to $4,729.80 while Bitcoin gave back $80,000 to $78,963, and yields eased a second session. NVDA reports into that split tomorrow at roughly 4:20 PM, eight hours after PCE.

Into tomorrow's open: the green line is software absorbing the INTU gap without generalizing — IGV down and bought while SOXX holds today's gain — a core PCE at or under ~3.3% that keeps the 10-year easing with crude near $81, and SPY closing above 770, which would finally put a damping mechanism under the tape before 4:20 PM. Own the front week outright and run no short gamma through tomorrow's close.
