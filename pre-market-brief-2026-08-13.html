Overnight Developments

What changed since yesterday's close — the soft PPI print and Cisco's margin-driven after-hours fade that reset the open.

1. July PPI came in soft — the wholesale confirmation of the cool CPI headline The 8:30 print landed cool: PPI flat MoM versus a +0.2% consensus, core (ex food/energy) +0.2% versus +0.3% est, and 4.7% YoY, down sharply from June's 5.5% and below the 4.9% expected. Coming a day after headline CPI cooled to 2.7%, it reinforces the easing-price read and lets the 10y bleed lower to ~4.67%. Constructive for the rate-sensitive book — though core CPI at a 5-month 3.1% yesterday keeps the September debate from being fully settled.
2. Cisco beat and raised AI orders to $9B — but fell ~4.6% AH on gross-margin worries CSCO reported Q4 FY26 rev $17.3B (+18%) and EPS $1.22 (+23%), both above guidance, and raised its FY AI hyperscaler order range to $9B (from $5B) with a strong FY27 outlook ($72.2-73.4B rev, $5.05-5.11 EPS, above Street). Yet shares fell ~4.6% AH to ~$118 as investors focused on gross-margin pressure from a heavier hardware mix. Read for the book's NOK leg: the AI-datacenter-networking secular demand is confirmed and accelerating, but the margin signal cools near-term momentum.
3. Applied Materials reports AH tonight — the direct DRAM/HBM capex tell AMAT reports Q3 after the close (~4:30 PM ET): consensus EPS $3.39, revenue ~$9B (+23% YoY). It is the marquee semicap read-through for the memory/HBM thesis after CRWV/SMCI validated AI backlog. Citi expects an above-Street October guide and UBS lifted its PT to $705 on equipment pricing power. AMAT trades ~$539, roughly 27% off its $739 peak — a reset bar that a firm WFE demand guide would re-underwrite for the DRAM leg.
4. Rates and oil stay contained — the cross-asset backdrop is cooperative The 10y eased to ~4.67% on the soft PPI, oil held contained (WTI ~$82.9, off recent highs as the Hormuz premium continues to drain), and VIX slipped to 14.66. The fading energy tax plus a lower yield is a direct tailwind for the rate-sensitive legs (APLD most, then DRAM/SKM). The swing risk is a fresh geopolitical incident that re-spikes crude, but this morning the cross-asset tape reads relief, not stress.
5. CALLOUT — Futures modestly green; VIX 14.66 keeps protection cheap into AMAT ES +0.2%, NQ +0.6%, YM ~flat, RTY +0.1% pre-open. VIX at 14.66 shows only marginal short-dated bid ahead of AMAT AH and Friday's Retail Sales/UMich — the tape isn't discounting a shock. Setup: let the disinflation-driven rate relief work the DRAM/APLD/SKM legs, but keep cheap optionality on into AMAT's guide and against the AI-infra margin signal Cisco just flagged.
