TL;DR — What You Need To Know

THE FED HELD A FIFTH STRAIGHT TIME - BUT HAWKISHLY. Warsh's FOMC left the funds rate at 3.50-3.75% (the fifth consecutive hold), but with no forward guidance, inflation still called 'too high', and September kept explicitly live. Hike odds had crept to ~31-38% into the print, so a hold is relief at the margin - but a hawkish-leaning one that defers rather than clears the rate overhang on the long-duration AI-infra leg (APLD).
IRAN'S STRIKE PUT THE OIL TAX BACK ON. Iran launched a surprise attack on a US base, reengaging the conflict after Friday's pause, and Brent jumped ~3-4% back toward $85. That reverses the prior week's crude relief and re-arms the energy-inflation channel the soft data had calmed - directly relevant the same day the Fed kept September live, and a live tailwind for the RDW defense/space hedge.
KOREAN MEMORY WAS ROUTED AGAIN ON 'RECORD BUT MISSED'. The Kospi fell ~6% and SK Hynix's ADR fell ~9% as its record Q2 - revenue 79.3T won, 76% operating margin, net profit +557% YoY, HBM4 now in mass production - still missed consensus, reviving fear the AI boom is slowing. This is the single most book-adverse read of the day: it hits SKM's Korean lane (worst-hit, shortest runway) and DRAM's memory cycle directly.
MSFT + META REPORTED AFTER THE BELL - THE CAPEX VERDICT IS THE SWING. Microsoft (fiscal Q4: Street ~$87.7B rev, ~$4.21-4.24 EPS, Azure guided 39-40% cc, FY27 capex the key swing near ~$220B+) and Meta (Q2: Street ~$60.2B rev, ~$7.13-7.18 EPS, 2026 capex $125-145B) dropped their guides - the direct answer to the circular-financing doubt. At publish (~7:05 PM ET) the after-hours price reactions were still being confirmed; they set Thursday's open into AAPL + AMZN + Q2 GDP.
