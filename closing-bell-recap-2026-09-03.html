Tomorrow's Setup — Three Paths Into the Open

Levels that matter: today's close at $765.16 is the pivot. Above $770 = continuation regime; below $760 = reversal regime; chop in between = pin mode.

Bull path
SNOW's raise re-rates the consumption layer, ISM services prices ease, and SPY trends off 765 toward 770

The bull path starts already partly funded. SPY settled 765.16 on a session where nine of eleven sectors closed green and the Russell led at +1.13%, and futures are marginally better this morning. The first trigger is SNOW's +22.4% after-hours re-rate spilling into the broader data and consumption complex — a 500bp raise to $6.07B on FY27 product revenue with a third consecutive quarter of accelerating growth is the cleanest fundamental datapoint of the AI cycle so far, and it argues that the workloads are real even where the capex is being questioned. The second is ISM Services at 10:00: prices paid easing to 69.5 from 70.3 would be the first evidence that the energy shock is not passing into services inflation, which takes pressure off the long end directly. The third is that the 10-year has already backed from 4.814% to 4.77% pre-open — the rout losing its momentum is worth more to this tape than any earnings print, because the Russell took two consecutive -1.23% sessions purely on duration and repairs fastest. Confirmation: a hold above 765 through the ISM print and a close above 767 with the 10-year under 4.78% and IWM green, which opens 770 and 773. Expression: keep the long in the consumption layer and in small caps, not in the capex names that just got sold on beats.

Base case
Dispersion without direction — 762-767 chop as AVGO and SNOW net out, with everything deferred to Friday 8:30

Base case and by some distance the most likely. The overnight tape gave both sides evidence and neither side a trend: SNOW +22.4% against AVGO -4% and HPE -4.1%, gold up 3.2% to $4,504 against ES futures up 0.11%, Brent back to $96.28 against the 10-year down to 4.77%. That nets to an index pinned while single names move in double digits, which is precisely what Wednesday's internals already looked like beneath the green screen. So the day holds its configuration: SPY chops 762-767, the 10-year sits 4.75-4.82%, Brent holds $94-98, gold holds above $4,450, and VIX stays pinned in the 15s with VIX9D under 13 because there is nothing on today's calendar large enough to reprice it. The information arrives in two places only: ISM Services at 10:00, then LULU, DOCU, ZS and CIEN after the close. Everything in between is netting. Posture: small in direction, long the front week, closing-basis levels only, and let tomorrow's 8:30 print do the work. The one thing not to do in a base case is sell the vol that is cheap for a mechanical reason rather than a fundamental one.

Bear path
AVGO fails to hold, gold's bid is telling the truth, or Friday's payroll lands soft with September pricing intact

Three independent triggers, and the tape needs only one. First, the reflexive one: AVGO grew AI semiconductor revenue 221% year-over-year to $16.7B and was still sold on a $250M shortfall against a $35B forward quarter. If it cannot hold its opening level today, the read is that the AI capex multiple has topped independent of fundamentals, and that cascades through the entire hardware and networking complex — HPE is already -4.1% and NTAP -9.4% on the same logic. Second, gold: a 3.2% overnight move to $4,504 with the dollar at 99.07 is the first genuine safe-haven bid of this episode, and it directly contradicts the equity repair. The bear resolution is that gold is right and the term-premium story is not finished — the G20 wrapped with no consensus amid the global bond selloff, and the move was always synchronized across gilts, bunds, OATs and JGBs rather than being a Fed event. Third, tomorrow: consensus +53K after July's -23K, and the dangerous outcome is not a weak print but a weak print that leaves 60-68% September pricing intact — that is stagflation and it takes the Russell that just led the bounce straight back down with no rate offset. The aggravator is structural and unchanged: VIX9D at 12.57 with no dealer long-gamma rebuilt since August expiration means a break travels rather than stalls. Beneath 762 the map is 759 then 757.
