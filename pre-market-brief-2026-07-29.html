Risk Matrix — Into the Open & Through the Session

1. MSFT/META capex reads as spend-without-return and the after-hours tape sells the hyperscalers themselves
High

If MSFT guides FY27 capex toward the feared $255-260B, or Meta raises 2026 capex without a matching monetization story - the exact reflex Alphabet just triggered - the after-hours tape sells the hyperscalers funding the buildout, not just the chipmakers, and the book's AI-infra legs (DRAM, SKM, APLD) re-rate on the financing question. The counterweight: Azure clearing ~40% and a backlog-anchored capex frame can just as easily re-underwrite the complex. But with the overnight tape already heavy (memory rout, oil re-spike) and a hawkish Fed risk at 2 PM, the downside is asymmetric if the guides disappoint - and it sets Thursday's open into AAPL/AMZN + GDP.
2. A Warsh-hawkish hold keeps September live and the oil re-spike re-taxes the long-duration book
High
A fifth hold is the base case, but the risk is the framing: with inflation 'too high', no forward guidance, and September kept live - the same day Iran's strike pushed oil higher and the 10Y to ~4.6% - the rate overhang is deferred, not cleared. If the 10Y turns further up off the decision, real rates matter for valuation again and the long-duration AI-infra leg (APLD on the $36-37 line) takes it first, compounding any capex disappointment into a genuine de-risk. Friday's PCE is the next inflation tell; the 10Y's path off 2 PM is the immediate read on whether the hold was dovish-enough or a hawkish deferral.
3. SK Hynix's 'record but missed' is a demand-slowing signal, not a low-bar wash-out - and the memory rout extends
Medium
SK Hynix delivered a record quarter (76% margin, HBM4 in mass production) and the complex still routed on a consensus miss and 'AI is slowing' framing - the bear read is that this is a genuine demand signal, not just positioning, and the memory rout extends rather than washes out. That directly hits DRAM's cycle and SKM (worst-hit leg, shortest runway), with Samsung's 7/30 data a second chance to confirm. The counter: a record off a crashed base with HBM4 shipping is a real result the bulls read as a low bar cleared. But the momentum is down and the China cheap-chip threat to the demand curve is a genuine overhang.
4. The value rotation gives way and the split tape converges down into the close
Medium
The day's saving grace is the split - value/consumer earnings and a ~18 VIX are holding the index up while the AI complex sells. The risk is that containment fails: if the Fed leans hawkish and MSFT/META's guides disappoint and the value bid can no longer offset, the rotation converts into a wholesale de-risk and the S&P loses the 740 trapdoor toward 737, dragging every leg down regardless of thesis. The tell is whether the cyclical/defensive complex holds through the 2 PM and 4 PM prints. The counter: a fifth hold and cash-flow-anchored value earnings are a real bid.
5. Iran re-escalation extends the oil spike and re-arms the energy-inflation channel
Low
The crosscurrent: Iran's surprise strike reengaged the conflict and pushed oil higher, lifting the 10Y toward ~4.6%. A durable re-escalation - a Hormuz or Red Sea headline - could extend the crude spike, re-arm the energy-inflation channel the Fed is about to flag, and re-tax the long-duration book (APLD) on top of the AI-financing overhang. The offset is that it re-bids the RDW defense/space hedge, so the book has a natural counterweight. But an oil spike layered on a capex disappointment and a hawkish hold would be a two-front pressure into Thursday.

The whole week's binary is compressed into one session with everything still ahead - a fifth Fed hold expected at 2 PM but hawkish-leaning as oil lifted hike odds to ~31-38%, and the MSFT/META/QCOM/ARM capex verdict at 4 PM - and the overnight tape leaned heavy going in (Iran's strike put the oil tax back on and lifted the 10Y to ~4.6%, SK Hynix's record Q2 still missed and routed memory, the Nasdaq sits on the brink of correction). Against that, three cushions remain: the tape stayed split rather than liquidating (value/consumer earnings and a ~18 VIX cushioning the index), the AI selloff is a valuation/financing re-pricing rather than a demand refutation (SK Hynix's 76% margin and HBM4 in mass production are a genuine record), and the two swing votes today can still arrest the rout if the Fed reads dovish-enough and the capex guides frame spend as demand. Net setup: the downside is asymmetric because the doubt is now the base case and the macro leaned heavy, but the split-and-rotation structure and the low-bar read on the memory print keep a sharp relief bounce live if the prints reassure.
