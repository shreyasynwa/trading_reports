Risk Matrix — Into the Open & Through the Session

1. Crude presses $100 and the 10-year takes out 4.814% with no equity hedge available
High
Brent is $97.45 — roughly $3 above where the 10-year printed 4.814%, its highest since November 2023. The causal chain has been demonstrated twice this week in both directions: crude up took yields to multi-year highs and the S&P down three straight sessions; crude stalling took the 10-year to 4.77% and produced Wednesday's +0.46%. Overnight the input reversed, and the posture is not de-escalatory — Iran struck Jordan, the UAE and Kuwait, Tehran claims 18 dead including four civilians at a wedding, and Trump called the retaliation a "love tap" while saying the US is "prepared to do another one anytime we want." The failure mode is treating flat futures as evidence the tape has absorbed this; it has not, it is being netted by one 24% earnings reaction. The genuine break level is Brent $100, last crossed on July 23 after tanker strikes, because that is where an inflation premium becomes a supply event twelve days before the FOMC. Instruction: use the 10-year as the confirming instrument on every equity decision — through 4.81% the Wednesday repair is void; under 4.80% it holds. Do not size long exposure off the index level while Brent is bid.
2. A yen through 155 on BoJ hike bets turns a rate story into a carry unwind, and nothing in equities hedges that
High
The yen jumped more than 1% overnight to 156.15, its strongest in a month, with USDJPY down 1.4% to 156.40, on raised bets for a Bank of Japan hike this month after hawkish policymaker comments. The BoJ decides September 18, two days after the FOMC, and the JGB 10-year already sits near a multi-decade high inside a bond selloff that has moved gilts, bunds, OATs and Treasuries together. This is the risk least represented in current positioning because it is not a Fed variable and does not appear in FedWatch at all. The mechanism is well understood and fast: rising JGB yields raise the return on domestic Japanese assets, yen appreciation impairs every yen-funded position abroad in the currency, and the resulting unwind hits leveraged duration and credit globally with no regard for equity technicals. That gold and bitcoin are rallying alongside the yen rather than falling says this is being read as a price-of-money event rather than a fear event — which is precisely why it can run without the usual warning signs. Instruction: watch USDJPY 155 as the regime line. Below it, treat equity weakness as a funding event — it will not stop at support and it will not stop on good news. Do not fund long equity risk with short vol into a BoJ meeting the market has barely discounted.

The configuration to fear tomorrow is a weak payroll print that leaves September pricing intact — that takes small caps and cyclicals down with no offset from rates and no rotation available.
