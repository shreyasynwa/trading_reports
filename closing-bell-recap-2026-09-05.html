TL;DR — What You Need To Know

THE PRINT WAS MARKED IN RATES AND IGNORED IN EQUITY VOL. Eight hundredths of a vol point for a 3x consensus surprise is not calm; it is a market that ran out of session before it ran out of work.

August CPI on Friday, September 11 is the last input before the September 15-16 FOMC, with Apple's event on the 9th, Oracle fiscal Q1, Adobe, PPI and the ECB on the 10th in between. Tomorrow's Setup — Three Paths Into the Open

Levels that matter: today's close at $765.16 is the pivot. Above $770 = continuation regime; below $760 = reversal regime; chop in between = pin mode.

Bull path
The deferral is validated — CPI cools, the 60% unwinds, and 773 is reclaimed on the first Tuesday session

The bull path does not need the payroll number reversed; it needs it not decisive, and Friday's close is technically consistent with that. The S&P gave back only 0.38% and finished the week +0.1%, the Nasdaq was +0.4% on the week, the Russell closed green at 2,974.85, and Technology, Industrials and Utilities held gains on a hawkish day — a tape that absorbed a 3x consensus surprise without a single sector losing more than 1.3%. The mechanism is Chair Warsh's own stated test: "we must be confident that underlying inflation is moving to our objective, clearly and at sufficient speed" — an inflation test, not an employment test, which routes the entire decision to August CPI on Friday, September 11. Waller has flagged a possible significant drop in core once revisions land and AI-driven disinflation. The demand side genuinely supports it: ISM services 55.4 with new orders at a three-and-a-half-year high of 60.9, PLTR +8%, CRWD +6% and NOW +5-7% on Friday, and a payroll diffusion index at 55.6 that removes the recession tail outright. Confirmation into Tuesday: a reclaim of 773 with the 10-year holding under 4.75% and IWM green a second session, opening 776 and 779. Expression: the broadening that keeps failing to arrive — IWM and equal-weight over more QQQ — because the small-cap complex has taken almost none of the last two moves in either direction and has the most to recover if the 60% unwinds on a soft CPI.

Base case
A 768-773 blackout-week range as desks refuse to add risk before the September 11 CPI

Base case and comfortably the most likely. The structure is a market that took a genuine hawkish shock, marked it in rates and FX, declined to mark it in equity vol, and then closed for ninety hours. The Fed blackout began Saturday, September 5 and runs through September 17, so no official can move the 58-60% in either direction, and the only scheduled input before the September 15-16 vote is August CPI on Friday, September 11. That is a full week of carry with a single binary at the end of it, which historically produces range and dispersion rather than trend. Expect SPY to chop 768-773 around the 770.3 anchor, the 10-year 4.72-4.82%, WTI $88-93 after a near-9% week, and VIX 14-16 grinding higher into the print rather than spiking. Underneath, dispersion stays extreme — GWRE -22.2%, LULU -17.5%, FICO -16.6% against SNDK +10% and PLTR +8% on an index that moves four-tenths — with Apple's September 9 event and Oracle's fiscal Q1 on September 10 supplying the only single-name volatility that matters. Posture: small in direction, long front-week convexity through the blackout rather than into it, closing-basis levels only, and let CPI on the 11th and the FOMC on the 15-16th be the events rather than any Tuesday or Wednesday tape.

Bear path
Rates finish the job — 4.81% goes, the megacap cushion fails, and 768 breaks with no gamma underneath

The bear path requires no new information, only follow-through on what Friday already established. September hike odds sit at 58-60% from 49%, the 10-year closed 4.79% against a 4.818% multi-year high printed Wednesday — three basis points of room — and the dollar has turned. Equities marked four-tenths of that. The aggravators are structural and specific to the week ahead. No meaningful dealer long-gamma has been rebuilt since August expiration, so a break of 768 travels rather than stalls, opening 765 then the 764 trapdoor. VIX at 14.40 means essentially nothing is pre-hedged. The Fed blackout through September 17 removes the verbal circuit-breaker that has capped every hawkish repricing since Jackson Hole — there is no Waller sentence available this time. The index cushion is dangerously thin: nine of eleven sectors closed red and the tape was held up by PLTR +8%, CRWD +6% and NOW +5-7%, so a single megacap disappointment at Apple on September 9 or Oracle on September 10 removes it, and GWRE -22.2% on a forward guide against a Q4 beat is the template for how that gets priced. Cross-asset agrees: WTI ended the week up nearly 9% above $90 with record diesel and ISM prices paid 72.6, feeding the exact CPI that now decides the meeting. And the seasonal offers nothing — since 1928 the S&P has fallen roughly 55% of the time in September, averaging -1.1%, with the damage concentrated in the second half, which is precisely the fortnight CPI on the 11th and the FOMC on the 16th occupy.

Bottom Line

The session story: Friday was the day the market marked a hawkish print everywhere except in equity vol. Payrolls at +162,000 against +53,000 with a 55.6 diffusion index moved the September hike from roughly 49% to 58-60%, lifted the 10-year about three basis points to 4.79%, and turned the dollar after a week at five-month lows — and the S&P gave back 0.38% to 7,718.60, the Dow 271.86 points to 53,414.25, the Nasdaq 0.29% to 26,506.99. Underneath, the tape was more honest than the headline: nine of eleven sectors closed red, with Thursday's rate-relief leaders giving back most (Consumer Discretionary -1.26%, Financials -0.76%), and the index held up by a narrow enterprise-software bid — PLTR +8%, CRWD +6%, NOW +5-7%, SNDK +10%. The Russell's green 2,974.85 close is the absence of selling in a cohort that never bought Thursday, not the arrival of breadth. The after-hours cascade that would normally re-rate all of this never came: no S&P 100 name reported tonight and there were no AH moves above 3%, so Friday's dispersion — GWRE -22.2%, LULU -17.5%, FICO -16.6%, TSN -7% — is frozen exactly as it closed. The week netted +0.1% on the S&P and a completely different Fed.

Into Tuesday's open: the market is carrying a 58-60% hike lean, VIX at 14.40, and no dealer gamma cushion across a ninety-hour close into a Fed blackout that runs through September 17 with August CPI on the 11th as the only thing that can move it. Own front-week SPY and QQQ convexity through CPI, size direction small, use the 10-year through 4.81% as the confirming break, and carry no single-name binary across the holiday.
