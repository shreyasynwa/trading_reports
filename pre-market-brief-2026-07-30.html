3. Apple disappoints on margins amid memory-cost pressure and the 'AI-safety' offset cracks
Medium
Apple has been the cash-flow 'AI-safety' bid offsetting the hyperscaler capex doubt. The risk is that rising memory-chip costs squeeze gross margins, or that iPhone/Services or China guidance underwhelms lofty expectations, removing the one clean mega-cap anchor at exactly the moment the tape needs it — with a ~$170B implied swing on the print. That would drag the broad index even if Amazon's AWS is fine, because Apple's weight and its role as the season's steadying capstone are outsized. 4. The futures rebound is a dead-cat bounce and the split tape converges back down
Medium
Today's bid rests on MSFT's relief and a cooling VIX, not on any resolution of the bond rout or the capex doubt. The risk is that the relief fades intraday: if PCE runs hot, the 30Y presses higher, and Amazon's capex sells after the close, the narrow MSFT-led leadership can't hold the index and the rotation converts into a broad de-risk that loses the 726 trapdoor toward 723, dragging every leg down regardless of thesis. The tell is whether breadth holds through the 8:30 data. The counter: a cooler VIX and a genuine Azure-demand signal are a real floor.
5. Iran de-escalation reverses and the energy-inflation channel re-fires on top of the bond rout
Low
Oil fell ~5% on Iran's conditional halt signal, but the de-escalation is fragile. A re-escalation — a Hormuz or Red Sea headline — could re-spike crude, re-arm the energy-inflation channel the Fed is already flagging, and re-tax the long-duration book (APLD) on top of the bond rout and any capex disappointment.
