What Happened Today

The bond market did all the work today; equities just tried not to look at it.

1. 10Y to 5.19%, 30Y to 5.48% — both multi-decade highs. Yesterday's PMI shock never stopped running: the 10-year added another ~8bp to 5.19% (highest since 2007) and the 30-year rose to 5.48% (highest since 2004), with the 2-year at 4.92%. Soft Treasury auction demand plus firm data pushed rate-hike odds higher instead of the cut path the market spent the summer pricing — a genuine regime flip that keeps multiples under pressure into Friday's PCE.
2. SPX -0.07% masks a 3-of-11-sector session. SPX/Dow/Nasdaq/IWM all closed within a third of a percent of flat, but only Communication Services (+1.08%), Energy (+0.86%, on crude's push toward $99) and Health Care (+0.50%) were green. Cyclical and rate-sensitive cohorts absorbed the bond move instead of the index — a rotation, not a reprieve.
3. Oracle, TD Synnex and Dropbox took the hit the index didn't. Oracle fell 4-6% after issuing a force-majeure notice to Blue Owl Capital on the $50B+ Project Jupiter data center — part of the Stargate build-out with OpenAI and SoftBank — reviving AI-capex-timeline anxiety even as both sides called it standard contract language. TD Synnex dropped 9.87% despite beating Q3 (EPS $5.68 vs $4.64 est., revenue $21.6B vs $18.79B) on pure valuation exhaustion after an 80% 12-month run, and Dropbox fell 4.5-4.9% on a Citi downgrade to Sell plus the same rate-driven software de-rating hitting duration names everywhere.
Sentiment is worse than the tape: CNN Fear & Greed slipped to an estimated ~28 (Fear) from 35 this morning — the internals are catching down to the bond move even where price hasn't.

Bottom Line

Into tomorrow: 10Y at 5.19% and 30Y at 5.48% are both fresh multi-decade highs into a PCE / durable-goods / UMich morning. A hot core print extends the break and generalizes tonight's idiosyncratic damage (Oracle, TD Synnex, Dropbox) into the broader tape; an in-line print is the only path back to 768-773.
