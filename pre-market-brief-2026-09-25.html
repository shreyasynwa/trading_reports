Risk Matrix — Into the Open & Through the Session

1. UMich confirms a real consumer break, not just a rates story
High
The preliminary University of Michigan read cratered 7.5% to 47.8 — the second-lowest on record — with 1-year inflation expectations at 4.6%. If the final print confirms rather than revises this away, it's evidence of genuine demand deterioration underneath a market that's been treating this cycle as purely a bond story.
2. October FOMC hike (~69% priced) keeps the long end from rolling over
High
The Fed hiked to 3.75-4.00% on Sept 16 and signaled at least one more move this year. Every data point between now and late October gets read through that lens first — today's pause in yields is a breather inside a hiking cycle, not a change of direction.
3. Hormuz talks stall or break down
Med
The entire oil fade and part of this morning's equity bid rests on reported progress in New York negotiations that have not produced an actual deal. A stalled or collapsed round of talks reverses WTI's move and removes the cleanest risk-on input on the board.
4. AI-capex-timeline anxiety spreads past Oracle and TD Synnex
Med
Oracle's force-majeure notice on Project Jupiter and TD Synnex's cash-drain-driven selloff are both being read as tells rather than one-offs. A third headline in the same vein this week would generalize the damage into the broader data-center and hyperscaler-supplier complex.
5. Costco's fade despite a clean beat is a demand signal, not noise
Med
A +9.4% comp, +19.5% digital quarter that can't hold even a modest after-hours pop is itself informative. If genuinely strong consumer data can't get a bid with rates here, the bar for today's economic releases to move the tape higher is lower than it looks.

The skew is still to the downside, but this morning's pause has narrowed it slightly: a stalled bond selloff and fading oil are giving equities real, if fragile, room to work higher. That only holds until 10:00 ET — a confirmed UMich collapse paired with firm durable goods flips the asymmetry back fast.
