Tomorrow's Setup — Three Paths Into the Open

Levels that matter: today's close at $765.53 is the pivot. Above $771 = continuation regime; below $761 = reversal regime; chop in between = pin mode.

Bull path
ROST holds its gap, BJ's corroborates, and SPY reclaims 766 on the expiration close

Bull path: ROST opens near its +7% AH mark and holds it, BJ's delivers membership-fee growth and a respectable comp, and the market completes the arithmetic — Walmart's 2.6% was ~80bps of legislated pharmacy caps plus self-inflicted price cuts, while off-price is accelerating on traffic. XLY leads, XLP stabilizes without needing to lead, and the consumer stops being a source of supply. Expiration pins and then resolves higher: SPY reclaims 766 on the close and opens the path to 770. Expression: XLY over XLP, IWM over SPY, and buy the off-price complex rather than chasing the mass merchant's dead-cat.

Base case
Pinned between 762 and 766 as expiration flows dominate, with the consumer split unresolved into Jackson Hole

Base case: monthly expiration does what expiration usually does. Decaying open interest holds SPY inside a 762-766 band, the futures' +0.3% open gets absorbed rather than extended, and the day's actual information arrives in single names — ROST gaps up and gives some back, BJ's lands ambiguous, WMT stabilizes without repairing. The 10y holds 4.66-4.74% with the buyback bid and the fiscal-supply story fighting to a draw, and the flash PMIs pass without incident. Crypto consolidates a 23% week. The index heads into the weekend having answered neither the consumer question nor the AI-financing question, with Bessent's Iran press conference Monday, NVDA Wednesday and Warsh Friday all in front of it. Posture: be small, let the expiration clear, and note the one structural change that is not optional — after today's bell the long-gamma cushion that has damped this entire range is gone, which makes Monday mechanically more volatile than anything August has produced.

Bear path
The ROST gap gets sold, BJ's confirms Walmart, and SPY loses 762 with the buyback bid already dead

Bear path: the market declines to credit ROST because roughly $0.60 of the EPS beat is tariff refunds — the same composition the tape punished in WMT and TGT — and a high-20s multiple after a $208-to-$255 run gives the gap nothing to hold. BJ's lands on Walmart's side with soft comps or decelerating membership income, and the consumer becomes a two-out-of-three problem rather than a one-off. Simultaneously the rates side offers no help: the buyback rally has already round-tripped to 4.696% and 5.24%, so there is no duration relief left to give, and a hot services PMI at 9:45 pushes the 30y back toward 5.30% into a Jackson Hole the Fed has every reason to use hawkishly. Bessent's Monday Iran presser becomes a weekend-risk headline rather than a catalyst. That is the configuration where expiration stops pinning and starts venting: SPY loses 762, targets 758 with 754 beneath, realized correlation normalizes off a level break, and a mid-15s VIX catches up in one move — with the dealer cushion dissolving at the same bell.

Bottom Line

The session story: Thursday was two sessions with opposite conclusions, and the market has only paid for one of them. The cash tape was a Walmart tape: WMT -9.15% to $103.84 on US comps +2.6% against a 3.5-3.8% bar — weakest since 2020, ticket +1.1% versus +3.1%, roughly 80bps of Medicare drug-cap drag, a 750 basis point tariff-refund benefit propping adjusted operating income and a Q3 guide below consensus — which took the Dow down 703.84 points (-1.32%) to 52,759.21 almost single-handedly, with the S&P -0.87% to 7,641.16, the Nasdaq Composite -1.00% to 26,067.17, the Russell 2000 -0.69% to 3,012.01 and VIX +5.5% to 15.71. Underneath it, the week's structural story failed its first test: the Treasury confirmed $4B+ per operation of 10- to 30-year buybacks from September 9 through November 4 and Bessent said on the record that "yields don't reflect the underlying fundamentals" and that he would go further — and the market returned the 10y to 4.696% and the 30y to 5.24% inside a session, erasing the entire rally and taking IWM's opening +0.5% down to -0.69% with it. Then the bell rang and the conclusion inverted. Ross Stores printed EPS $2.66 against $1.94 on $6.3B of revenue with comparable sales +10% driven by traffic, guided Q3 comps to +6-7% against a ~3% Street and Q4 to +4-5%, raised FY EPS to $8.61-8.77, and reversed a red cash day to trade +7.05% at $245.15 after hours — the direct rebuttal to Walmart, arriving on the same page. Off-price accelerating on traffic while the mass merchant decelerates on ticket is a share-shift signature, not a spending break, which reframes the day from a consumer verdict into a XLY-over-XLP trade. Friday opens green — ES +0.26%, NQ +0.38%, RTY +0.48%, VIX 15.83 — into BJ's before the bell, flash PMIs at 9:45 and August monthly expiration, with SPY resting on 762.78, the cushion still overhead at 770, and the whole thing dissolving at the close.

Into today's open: the green line is ROST holding its +7% gap into the close, BJ's corroborating with firm membership-fee income and comps, a contained services PMI that keeps the 10y under 4.72%, XLY leading XLP, IWM converting its +0.48% pre-open, and SPY settling back above 766.
