TL;DR — What You Need To Know

NVDA DID NOT JUST CLEAR THE BAR — IT MOVED IT. The four-print losing streak is over.
THE BIFURCATION THESIS BROKE — SOFTWARE RALLIED WITH HARDWARE, NOT AGAINST IT. Yesterday's framework said the software complex was the bear case for NVDA one layer downstream. The tape refuted it inside three hours: CRM +12% on adjusted EPS $5.90 vs $3.27, Agentforce ARR >$1.5B (+240%), 3.2B agentic actions (+97% q/q) and an FY27 raise to $46.1-46.4B; OKTA +20.9% on a raise to $3.216-3.226B; CRWD +10% with FY27 to $5.99-6.01B; NTNX +5%. INTU was a company problem, not a sector verdict. The IGV-versus-SOXX regime call is invalidated — do not carry it into today.
THE REAL FAULT LINE IS MEMORY COST, AND IT SPLITS HARDWARE — NOT SOFTWARE. NVDA guided gross margin down to 74% in Q3 and a 71-72% trough in Q4, explicitly citing memory prices that exceeded expectations and are still rising into next year. On the same tape, HPQ fell 11% despite beating on both lines and raising full-year targets, purely on a Q4 margin guide hit by memory and display costs. That is the same input landing on two different balance sheets. NVDA can absorb 300-400bp of margin because it is compounding revenue at triple digits; the assemblers and anyone without pricing power cannot. Screen the AI supply chain by who sets price, not by who ships boxes.
THE GAP IS NARROW AND THE VOL IS ALREADY GONE. ES +0.37% to 7,718.50 and NQ +0.89% to 29,550.75, but YM -0.09% and RTY -0.07% — two mega-caps carrying an index with nothing beneath them, off a cash session that closed 30 advancing against 57 declining (34.5%). Tomorrow's Setup — Three Paths Into the Open

Levels that matter: today's close at $766.08 is the pivot. Above $771 = continuation regime; below $761 = reversal regime; chop in between = pin mode.

Bull path
The gap holds and broadens — SPY takes 770 on a close, breadth confirms, and the vol crush finally has something to work on

Bull path needs one thing the overnight tape does not yet have: participation. ES +0.37% and NQ +0.89% imply SPY opens near 769, right at the level that matters. In the bull case the open is bought rather than faded, 770 is taken and held into the close, and the advance-decline line inverts from Wednesday's 30-against-57 to something better than one-to-one by mid-morning. The mechanism is available: 770 is the gamma strike that sat unoccupied all month, and reclaiming it on a closing basis flips dealer hedging from amplifying to damping — which would put a genuine cushion under the tape before Warsh Friday rather than after. The fundamental support is now unusually broad for a single night's work: NVDA's $108B Q3 guide with China at zero and a ~70% FY28 framing settles the demand question through next year, and CRM +12%, OKTA +21% and CRWD +10% mean the software complex re-rates alongside rather than funding it. MRVL confirming $3B for Q3 tonight would extend it into custom silicon and drag AVGO with it into next week's print. Above 770 the magnets are 774 and 778. Expression: hold semis and quality software into strength, add on a broadening tape rather than a higher NQ, and keep the front-week convexity through Friday regardless — it is the hedge, not the position, and it is not what you sell to pay for the rally.

Base case
Gap-and-fade to a hold — SPY opens near 769, works 766-772, and the index waits for MRVL and Warsh

Base case is the most likely and the least satisfying. SPY opens near 769 on the futures, sells the first thirty minutes as the overnight longs take the gap, then holds a band between 766 and 772 without resolving. The reason is structural: the gap is two names wide — YM -0.09% and RTY -0.07% are not participating, and Wednesday's 34.5% advance ratio means there is no bench to bring on. Semis and quality software stay bid without extending, HPQ and the assembler complex stay heavy on the memory-cost read, healthcare continues to leak after XLV -1.00%, and discretionary stays split between ANF's +35.7% and the DKS/TGT/KSS damage. Nobody sizes up before MRVL at ~4:05 PM into a 12.4% implied move, and nobody sizes down before a Fed chair. Cross-asset holds its overnight configuration: WTI $81-84, gold $4,600-4,700, 10-year 4.62-4.72%, Bitcoin working the $78-80k shelf. VIX is the interesting instrument in this path — at 14.99 it has no room to crush further and every reason to firm into the last hour ahead of tonight's prints and Friday's keynote, which makes a drifting, green, low-volume afternoon the best entry the week will offer for front-week convexity. Posture: small in direction, respect 766 and 770 on a closing basis only, and let the calendar do the work.

Bear path
The gap fails and fills — 766 goes on a close, the memory-margin read spreads, and 762 comes into play with no cushion

Bear path does not require the earnings to have been bad; it requires the gap to be as narrow as it looks. Trigger one: SPY opens near 769, fails to hold 770, and closes back under 766 — a full gap fill on a day when the news was as good as it gets, which is the most bearish single outcome available and would say the tape is out of buyers rather than out of reasons. Trigger two is fundamental and specific: the market re-reads NVDA's gross margin guide down to a 71-72% Q4 trough as the story rather than the footnote, with HPQ -11% on a beat as the proof of concept. If DELL and HPE get sold in sympathy into their Sep 1-2 prints, that becomes a supply-chain-wide margin de-rating that a demand beat does not answer. Trigger three: MRVL confirms $3B and sells off anyway on a 12.4% implied move into a stock +196% YTD — the clearest possible signal that good news is fully priced across AI hardware. The aggravator is unchanged and now cuts the other way: the dealer long-gamma cushion settled away at August expiration and a gap this size does not rebuild it, so a failure at 770 travels rather than stalls. Below 766 the map is 762, then 758 as the trapdoor with 754 beneath and no mechanism between — and it would arrive into Warsh Friday at 10:00 AM with VIX starting from 14.99 and MOVE at 69.44, meaning neither market is carrying any premium for a hawkish surprise.
