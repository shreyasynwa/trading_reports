Gap down on the AH tape, 770 gets tested, and the index chops 766-774 waiting on the keynote

Bear path
770 fails, the NVDA session is given back through 766, and a hawkish keynote finds no cushion beneath 762

The bear path does not require bad news; it requires the advance to have been as narrow as it measurably was. Trigger one is mechanical: SPY opens under 770 on the after-hours tape and fails to reclaim it, which un-flips dealer hedging back to amplifying on a day the market has no earnings engine left — NVDA, CRM, CRWD, MRVL, WDAY, ADSK and AFRM have all printed. Trigger two is Warsh declining to endorse the 69% neutral consensus into core PCE at 3.3%, a 9-3 split and one-in-three September hike odds, with VIX in the mid-14s carrying no premium and the bond market carrying none either. In that configuration the level that matters is 766, because it is where the entire NVDA session is given back on a stock that added $435B; losing it would say the single best print of the cycle bought the index nothing, which is a materially worse signal than any individual guide. Beneath it, 762 is the pivot that held twice last week and 758 is the trapdoor, with no long-gamma damping anywhere in between since August expiration and the equal-weight index already negative before any of it starts.
