Risk Matrix — Into the Open & Through the Session

1. AI-capex sticker shock broadens from AMD/SpaceX into the whole infra complex
High
The dominant risk is that the market's decision to punish AI capex — AMD -~8% on ~$808M vs ~$314M expected, SpaceX -~11% on an ~$18.4B quarter — bleeds from the two names into the broader AI-infra complex the book rides (DRAM, SKM, APLD), overriding a genuinely strong demand read. It would land on a tape at record highs with a VIX only just off a one-year low. The counter: green futures, Nvidia's partner-demand bid and Shopify's surge say the market is treating the fades as idiosyncratic spend fumbles for now, and the underlying build-out demand is intact.
2. A hot ISM Services print lifts the 10Y into a capex-jittery infra tape
High
ISM Services PMI (July) at 10 AM into a 10Y already near ~4.7% is the compounding risk: a hot services number lifts yields into an AI-infra complex that is simultaneously digesting the capex fear, re-taxing the long-duration legs (APLD most sensitive) just as sentiment is fragile. The counter: a soft-but-not-weak print is the base case that lets green futures carry, and the labor capstone (payrolls) doesn't land until Friday, so one benign services number keeps the melt-up intact.
3. The VIX off a one-year low means any disappointment re-prices with little cushion
Medium
The VIX at ~16.5 is up off Tuesday's one-year low but still near the floor. The risk is mechanical: with realized and implied both compressed, a broadening capex worry, a hot ISM, a weak Disney/Arista print or a Hormuz breakdown lands on a tape priced near perfection and can trigger an outsized vol re-rating. The counter: the melt-up is earnings-backed and broad, so a vol pop is more likely a shakeout than a regime change — which is exactly why cheap protection is worth carrying rather than chasing.
4. SpaceX's ~$109B lock-up pressures the space complex and the RDW hedge
Medium
The staged lock-up freeing ~911M shares (~$109B) two trading days out is a dated supply overhang that can pressure the entire listed space complex regardless of operating results, and read through to RDW even with its own DoD/NASA/NATO catalysts intact. The counter: it is a known, scheduled event the market can digest, and RDW's contracted defense revenue is idiosyncratic rather than tied to the SpaceX P&L — a near-term headwind, not a fundamental shock.
5. Disney/Arista prints miss and feed the spend-worry narrative
Low
Disney pre-market (Street ~$1.20 / ~$23.1B, with park-attendance softness a known concern) and Arista post-close are today's earnings swings. A Disney miss on consumer softness or an Arista guide that flags AI-networking spend could feed the day's capex/spend narrative and narrow breadth. The counter: Disney has beaten four straight quarters, Shopify already surged, and a broad set of beats broadens the tape and blunts the AMD/SpaceX capex read — the more likely outcome absent a shock.

The open is a genuine two-sided setup: futures hold green off record highs, but the overnight narrative flipped from 'beats extend the melt-up' to 'how much are the AI leaders spending.' Three cushions are real — the demand read under AMD's and SpaceX's fades is intact (data-center beat, AI revenue +247%), Nvidia's partner-demand bid and Shopify's surge keep the tape broad, and a firmer-but-contained crude keeps the inflation tail dormant. But the asymmetry has shifted: the market is now repricing AI capex, so the live swing is whether that fear stays boxed in AMD/SpaceX or de-rates the whole infra complex the book rides (DRAM, SKM, APLD) — and it does so with a VIX only just off a one-year low, into an ISM Services print that could lift the 10Y (~4.7%) onto the long-duration legs, and with the SpaceX ~$109B lock-up overhang on RDW two days out. Net setup: carry the confirmed cycle on closing-basis lines, keep cheap protection on into ISM Services and the cash open, and treat a broadening of the capex fear — not the two single-name fades themselves — as the signal that the melt-up's first real test has arrived.
