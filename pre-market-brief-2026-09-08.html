Risk Matrix — Into the Open & Through the Session

1. The yen carry unwind becomes a cross-asset liquidation rather than an FX move
High
USD/JPY has fallen from roughly 160 to 152.89 in under a week — the yen's strongest since February and a ~4.5% appreciation in a funding currency. The carry crowd took on excessively large positions and the unwinding is now accelerating, driven by repatriation flow, US pressure, and a BoJ that markets have almost fully priced for 25bp to 1.25% on September 17-18. The mechanism is indiscriminate: leveraged positions are closed regardless of whether the underlying view is intact, and the cohort that pays is whatever is most crowded — which today is the narrow megacap and enterprise-AI complex holding the index up. A stronger yen coinciding with rising global long-end yields removes liquidity from both legs at once, and bitcoin at $78,000 is already confirming. Instruction: track USD/JPY as a risk instrument, not a currency quote. Through 152 the unwind accelerates and high-beta pays first — reduce gross rather than hedging delta. Above 155 it was positioning noise and the tape can trade the oil story on its own terms. Carry no leverage across the FOMC-then-BoJ sequence.
