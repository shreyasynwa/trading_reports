3. Low VIX at record highs — complacency amplifies any reversal
Medium
VIX near ~15.6 with the index at fresh highs is thin protection against a single-name or macro shock. The risk is that the AMD print or a 30Y push triggers a reversal into rich positioning, and the low-vol complacency amplifies it as hedges get bid and momentum unwinds — a sharper move than the calm tape implies. The tell is whether breadth holds through the after-close print. The counter: a genuine demand confirmation from AMD plus a contained long end is a real floor, and the PLTR/CAT signals argue the bid has substance.
4. Oil re-spikes as US-Iran/Hormuz breaks down and the energy-inflation channel re-arms
Medium
Crude recovered toward ~$81 on unresolved Hormuz, and the de-escalation is fragile — Iran denies direct talks even as Oman-mediated shipping discussions progress. A breakdown or a Hormuz headline could re-spike crude, re-arm the energy-inflation channel the Fed is already flagging, and re-tax the long-duration book on top of the 30Y overhang. The offset is that it re-bids the RDW defense/space hedge. A crude spike layered on the rate overhang would be two-front pressure; the current stabilization softens the RDW premium.
5. Friday payrolls run hot and harden the September-hike path into a rich tape
Low
The week's macro capstone is Friday's jobs report, and the tail risk is that a hot payrolls print hardens the ~65%-priced September hike, pressures the 30Y further, and gives a record-priced, low-VIX tape a delayed reason to fade after AMD. The long-duration legs (APLD, DRAM, SKM) would take the rate re-pricing. The counter: a cooling labor read is the relief valve that validates the melt-up and lets the long end ease — but the setup has to clear both AMD tonight and payrolls Friday.
