Tomorrow's Setup — Three Paths Into the Open

Levels that matter: today's close at $767.45 is the pivot. Above $772 = continuation regime; below $762 = reversal regime; chop in between = pin mode.

Bull path

Base case
Choppy repair below 770 — rotation continues, semis stay heavy, and nothing resolves before Friday's opex

Base case: SPY spends the session between 766 and 770, unable to reclaim the line it lost, while the rotation that defined Tuesday persists — Energy, Healthcare and Staples absorbing the flows leaving Technology. The minutes at 2:00 read modestly hawkish but backward-looking enough to be discounted, the 10y stays in a 4.68-4.75% band, and semis chop without repairing because nobody wants to be long the group into NVDA on 8/26. The structural feature that matters is Friday's opex: with dealers' long-gamma cushion now sitting above a level the index has already lost, hedging flows stop damping and the range widens into expiration. Posture: hold the rotation, keep index size small, and let the 766 shelf do the deciding.

Bear path

Bear path: the 2:00 PM minutes show the three dissenters gaining ground against $91.87 Brent, the 10y reverses back through 4.75% and the 30y extends past 5.35%, and the two independent bear cases finally stack — a discount-rate shock landing on a complex whose cash-flow quality was just publicly questioned. SPY loses 766, 762 is the next shelf and 758 beneath, with Friday's opex removing what dampening remains. Tell: XLV and XLP giving back Tuesday's gains — when the hiding places sell, it is a market event.

Bottom Line

The session story: Tuesday was a de-rating of AI hardware that arrived through a beat, on a day when every macro variable moved the friendly way. The morning page argued a rising long end would mark down the AI complex. The long end did not cooperate — the 10-year eased to 4.706%, gold rallied 1.32% to $4,423.80, the dollar fell 0.22%, and OVX collapsed 10.87% to 47.17 even with Brent up to $91.87 — and the AI complex was marked down anyway, on its own operating merits. Fabrinet closed -19.4% at $482.59 after posting record fiscal Q4 revenue of $1.316B, +45% year over year, and non-GAAP EPS of $4.10 against $2.65, because investors looked past the print to datacom revenue down 1% sequentially on declining NVDA sales, sharply weaker free cash flow, a large inventory build and new borrowing to fund capex. The market generalized that immediately and without discrimination: COHR -12% and LITE -10% despite 59% and 109% datacenter growth of their own, MRVL -8%, APH -7%, CRDO -13.0%, AAOI -15.2%, MXL -14.3%, AXTI -14.2%, AEHR -15.4%, and memory and storage at SNDK -9%, STX -9%, WDC -7%. The SOX fell 5%. At the index level that produced SPX -0.69% to 7,691.76, Nasdaq -1.33% to 26,289.71, Dow -0.22% to 53,343.40 and IWM -1.26% — with XLE +1.76%, XLV +1.60%, XLP +1.06% and XLF +0.45% green, and XLC, XLY, XLU and XLRE essentially flat. That configuration is a rotation, and breadth agrees: 28 advancing against 59 declining, but a median stock down only 0.71% against an average of -1.29%, with 64.4% still above the 20-day. The after-hours cascade was conspicuously absent — no top-10 name reported, nothing moved 3%, and the strongest print of the night was KEYS beating by $0.59, which argues the equipment layer is not seeing what the component layer just priced. The one thing that genuinely deteriorated is the mechanics: SPY closed at 767.45, below the 770 gamma line, with VIX9D up 20.5% to 16.85 and now inverted above a spot VIX at 15.77 — the near-dated book finally paying for a calendar that runs through Friday's opex.

Into today's open: the green line runs through ADI confirming what KEYS said rather than what FN said, the SOX holding Tuesday's low, the 10y staying under 4.70% through the 2:00 PM minutes, TGT/LOW/TJX landing clean enough to keep the consumer question closed, and SPY reclaiming 770 on a closing basis. The red line is ADI echoing Fabrinet's cash-flow-and-inventory pattern, the 10y reversing back through 4.75% on hawkish minutes, TGT confirming what Klarna's -22.8% and LZB's 'uneven consumer' already suggested, XLV and XLP giving back Tuesday's gains — the hiding places selling is the tell that rotation has become liquidation — or SPY losing 766 into 762 with correlation normalizing and a 15.77 VIX catching up in one move.
