2. GOOGL -3.6% was the mega-cap drag — antitrust appeal + a $25B debt raise + capex angst Alphabet fell 3.6% on heightened antitrust-appellate risk (enforcers pressing to overturn search remedies and push Chrome/ad-tech divestitures), a freshly closed $25B senior-notes raise that expands leverage to fund AI data-center buildout, and investor anxiety over an upward capex revision pressuring near-term FCF/margins. The tell that the AI-capex bill is a two-edged story — even on a night the pure-play infra names beat.
3. Energy + healthcare led; real estate + technology lagged The rotation stayed defensive/oil-tilted: energy and healthcare were the only real sector leaders on the crude bid, while real estate and technology finished in the red under the rate pressure. Breadth was soft and event-gated, with the tape marking time into the AH earnings wave and CPI rather than making a directional statement of its own.
4. Vol slipped even as the tape fell — VIX 15.28, 10y >4.7% VIX eased to 15.28 (-1.16%) despite the red close — the market let protection cheapen into a two-binary night (AI-infra prints + CPI), a mild complacency flag. The 10y pushing above 4.7% on the oil run is the cross-asset pressure to respect: it kept the rate-sensitive book (APLD most) on the back foot and is the channel a hot CPI would extend.
5. CALLOUT — The AH earnings wave rescued the AI-infra setup CoreWeave (+8%), Super Micro (+8%) and Sea (+6%) all beat and popped after the bell. The two AI-infra names cleared the bar that Nvidia's fade and GOOGL's angst had thrown into question, reopening the AI-capex bid into Wednesday's open. The cash session closed red; the after-hours tape flipped the near-term setup constructive for the book's DRAM/APLD/SKM legs — pending CPI.
